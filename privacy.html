Introduction

On Harrison Accounting & Consulting Services ("On Harrison," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit this website, contact us, use our services, or participate in our text messaging (SMS) program.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: name, business name, mailing address, email address, and phone number (including mobile number)
  • Service information: details you provide about your accounting, tax, bookkeeping, payroll, or consulting needs
  • Communications: messages, emails, texts, and call notes exchanged with our team
  • SMS records: records of your consent to receive text messages, opt-out requests, and message history

This website does not use cookies or collect personal information from visitors.

Financial and tax information provided as part of a client engagement is handled under our engagement agreements and the confidentiality obligations that apply to accounting and tax professionals.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested services
  • Prepare proposals, estimates, and engagement letters
  • Schedule appointments and send reminders
  • Communicate with you about your engagement, deadlines, documents, and billing
  • Send text messages consistent with the consent you have given
  • Maintain records of SMS consent and opt-out requests
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and professional obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us operate our business (such as accounting and tax software, secure document portals, and communications platforms including RingCentral), solely to perform services on our behalf and subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Government agencies (such as the IRS or state tax authorities) when required to complete services you have engaged us for
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights, property, or safety

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Text Message (SMS) Communications

If you opt in to our SMS program, we may send you text messages about your inquiries, appointments, engagement updates, document requests, billing, and customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out. Reply HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Our messaging provider may process your phone number and message content solely to deliver text messages on our behalf.

For full program details, see our SMS Terms & Conditions.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain personal information as long as needed to provide services, maintain business and professional records, resolve disputes, and meet legal and regulatory retention requirements.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at info@onharrison.finance or (850) 832-5246. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal, regulatory, or professional record-keeping obligations. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services and this website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
