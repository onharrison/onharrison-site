1. Program Description

On Harrison Accounting and Consulting Services ("On Harrison," "we," "us," or "our") offers a text messaging (SMS) program so clients and prospective clients can communicate with our team by text. These SMS Terms and Conditions govern your participation in that program.

2. Consent to Receive Text Messages

You may opt in to receive text messages from On Harrison by checking the SMS consent box on our client intake or engagement form, by giving verbal or written consent to a member of our team, or by texting us first and confirming that you would like to receive replies by text. You will receive messages at the mobile number you provide.

Providing your phone number alone does not enroll you in our SMS program. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from us.

3. Types of Messages

Messages you may receive include:

  • Responses to your questions and service inquiries
  • Appointment scheduling, confirmations, and reminders
  • Requests for documents or information needed for your tax, bookkeeping, payroll, or consulting engagement
  • Notices about filing deadlines, engagement status, and completed work
  • Billing and invoice notifications
  • General customer care and follow-up on services you requested

We will not send sensitive information such as full Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or tax return contents by text message.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your inquiries, the services you receive, and your interactions with our team.

5. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on your mobile carrier and plan. Contact your wireless provider for details. We do not charge for our text messages.

6. How to Opt Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Reply STOP to any message from us (you may also reply STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT). After you send STOP, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive SMS messages from us unless you opt in again. To rejoin, reply START or contact us.
